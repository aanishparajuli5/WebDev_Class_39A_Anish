Introduction

James Carter is a Kathmandu-based technologist and the founder of SpeedRun Digital Solutions, a company known for building digital products for Nepali businesses. With over 12 years of experience in software engineering and entrepreneurship, he has grown the company from a two-person team in Thamel to one of the most recognized IT firms in Nepal.

His work on fintech and e-governance projects has been featured in national tech publications, including a case study published as Volume 3rd Edition, Chapter 5b. James believes that local problems deserve world-class solutions, and his approach has been called a blueprint for Nepali tech startups by industry peers. He continues to mentor young Nepali developers to this day.
